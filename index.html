AREA 01

Bank Debts & Long-Overdue Accounts

Debts with banks and institutions that haven't been paid for a long time. Set aside a monthly settlement fund to negotiate aggressive discounts (60%–85% haircut).

💡
Negotiation Strategy Tip:

For debts delinquent past 12–24 months, banks typically assign or write down debt. Propose a single cash lump-sum settlement from your dedicated fund for 20% to 35% of the initial principal during settlement campaigns (Feirão Limpa Nome, Black Friday, etc.).
